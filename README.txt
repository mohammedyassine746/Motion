# Motion 🖱️

### Your mouse. Your shortcuts. Your workflow.

**Motion** is a customizable Windows desktop application designed to transform the way you interact with your mouse.

Instead of relying on traditional mouse buttons and repetitive keyboard shortcuts, Motion aims to give you a more powerful and personalized way to control your workflow through mouse gestures, radial menus, custom actions, and application-specific profiles.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🖱️ Mouse Customization
- Configure mouse buttons and their actions.
- Support for additional mouse buttons where detectable.
- Customize how your mouse interacts with Windows and applications.

### 🎯 Radial Menus
- Interactive radial menus centered around your cursor.
- Nested menus for organizing actions.
- Customizable segments, icons, and layouts.
- Quick access to frequently used commands.

### 👆 Mouse Gestures
- Trigger actions through directional mouse movements.
- Configurable gesture buttons and directions.
- Customizable gesture behavior and sensitivity.

### ⚡ Smart Actions
- Build custom multi-step action sequences.
- Combine keyboard shortcuts, mouse actions, and application launching.
- Automate repetitive tasks.

### 📁 Application Profiles
- Create profiles for individual applications.
- Customize actions for different workflows.
- Switch configurations based on the active application.

### 🎨 Customization
- Modern interface with light and dark themes.
- Font Awesome icon integration.
- Customizable settings and interaction behavior.

---

## 🎯 Vision

Motion aims to make mouse interaction faster, more intuitive, and more customizable.

Whether you're working in design software, browsing the web, programming, or simply navigating Windows, Motion is being developed to put your most-used actions right at your fingertips.

The goal is simple: **less repetitive interaction, more control, and a workflow that adapts to you.**
